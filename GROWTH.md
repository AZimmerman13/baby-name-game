# StorkPool Growth & Monetization Plan

Last updated: 2026-09-18

## The shape of the business

Each pool recruits its own audience: one creator invites 10–40 participants, and many of
those participants are either expecting or close to someone who is. But each family needs
StorkPool roughly once per baby, so lifetime value is low and paid acquisition almost
certainly can't pay for itself. Growth has to come from **sharing, search, and Pinterest**.

The number that matters most: **participants who go on to create their own pool**
(`Guess → New Pool` on `/admin/stats`). Above ~30% word of mouth compounds on its own.

## Step 1 — Measurement and virality (done)

- **First-party analytics** (`backend/analytics.py`, `frontend/src/services/analytics.ts`).
  No third-party script, so ad blockers don't hide traffic. No IPs stored; visitors are
  counted with a hash that rotates daily. Pool IDs and admin tokens are stripped from paths.
- **Events:** pageview, Pool Created (with the CTA that caused it), Guess Submitted,
  Pool Revealed, Results Shared, Share Link Copied, Create CTA Clicked.
- **Dashboard** at `/admin/stats`, protected by `ADMIN_SECRET_KEY` (`X-Admin-Key` header).
  Pools/guesses/reveals are read from the real tables, so those counts are exact.
- **CTAs** after submitting a guess and on the results page; native share sheet on mobile;
  shared results text links to the results page (which carries a CTA).
- **SEO basics:** `robots.txt` (private pool/results pages disallowed) and `sitemap.xml`.

## Step 2 — Pinterest (in progress)

Baby shower planning lives on Pinterest, and pins keep sending traffic for months.

- **Lead magnet:** a printable prediction card sheet at `/printables/baby-prediction-cards/`
  — free, no email required, with "run it online instead" pointing at pool creation.
- **Pin images:** templates in `frontend/public/pins/` (1000×1500, Pinterest's 2:3 ratio),
  exported as PNG and uploaded manually.
- **Pin copy:** see `marketing/pinterest.md`.
- **Cadence:** a handful of pins per week beats a single burst. Use a business account so
  Pinterest analytics work, and tag every link with `?utm_source=pinterest`.

## Step 3 — SEO content pages (in progress)

Static HTML under `frontend/public/`, served directly by nginx — not React routes, because
the SPA renders client-side and search engines index it slowly and unreliably.

- **Guides** (`/guides/<slug>/`): baby pool template, office baby pool ideas,
  virtual baby shower games, baby due date pool.
- **Name pages** (`/names/<name>/`): "How popular is the name Olivia?" with a popularity
  trend chart from SSA data plus a CTA. This is the long-tail volume play.
  Generated by `frontend/scripts/generate-seo.js` from the SSA dataset.
- **Sitemap** is regenerated by the same script.

## Step 4+ — Monetization (not started)

Ordered by how well each fits what's already built:

1. **Affiliate links** (Babylist / Amazon Baby Registry) — pays per registry signup, keeps
   the product free. Best first revenue.
2. **Premium Pool, one-time $4.99–9.99** — themes, baby photo on the reveal, no StorkPool
   branding, printable winner certificate, keepsake PDF of every guess, extra categories.
   Parents pay for keepsakes, not for features.
3. **Gift fund** — participants chip in to a fund for the family, no prize attached, small
   platform fee via Stripe Connect.
4. **Office/team plans** — logo on the pool, several pools per account.
5. **Display ads** — low value at this traffic and cheapens a sentimental product.

### ⚠️ Do not build the 50/50 cash raffle without a lawyer

`local/FUTURE_FEATURES.md` describes paid entries with a cash prize split. Pay-to-enter +
prize + chance is generally an illegal lottery in most US states; raffles are usually
restricted to licensed nonprofits. The gift fund above achieves a similar feeling legally.

## Operating notes

- Tag every link you post with `?utm_source=` so `/admin/stats` can attribute it.
- Visit `/admin/stats` from your own browser once — it then excludes your own visits.
- Submit `sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console after each batch of new pages.
